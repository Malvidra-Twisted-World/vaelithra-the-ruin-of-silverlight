## Backstory

Daijoke inherited his father's workshop, where he was working as an assistant when he was young. Since taking over, he was having issues with lifting heavy machines and taking things from the top shelves. So he decides to build an Arcane Armor which he was able to synchronize with his movements to help him with the heavy lifting. He named it S.P.A.R.K.Y. (Synchronized Protective Armor with Reactive Kinesis, Y-Unit. The "Y" stand for "YES, IT WORKED!"). Daijoke also used S.P.A.R.K.Y. as a protective armor whenever trouble comes. With the help of S.P.A.R.K.Y., Daijoke continue to help people repair and create machines for a better life, and occasionally beat up bullies and criminals.
