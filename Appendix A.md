# Creatures & Monsters

## Vaeil

**Alignment:** Lawful Good

**Domains:** Time, Order, Creation

**Pantheon:** The Primordials

**Symbol:** A serene, all-seeing eye within a perfect clock, surrounded by the Ouroboros.

**Vaeil** is the benevolent deity who brought order from primordial chaos and established the flow of time. He is the guardian of the timeline, ensuring that the grand design of the cosmos unfolds as it should.

While **Vaeil** is often seen as a distant and impassive force, his influence is felt in the cycles of the seasons, the turning of the stars, and the natural progression of life and death. His followers, such as the **Acolyte of Silver Light**, strive to maintain order, preserve knowledge, and protect the world from the disruptive influence of chaotic forces.

**Vaeil**'s power is not one of direct intervention, but of subtle guidance and correction. He acts through his devoted followers to restore balance and mend the tears in the fabric of reality caused by beings like **Xyloth**. His greatest triumph was the ending of the *War of Sundering*, which he accomplished by resetting time, an act that fractured a part of his soul, creating the entity known as **Vaelithra**.

***Change Shape.*** Vaelithra can use her action to polymorph into a Medium humanoid she has seen, or back into her true form. Her statistics, other than her size, are the same in each form. Any equipment she is wearing or carrying isn't transformed. She reverts to her true form if she dies.  
In her humanoid form, she appears as a striking but unassuming human woman with silver-streaked hair and eyes that occasionally flicker with an inner light. This form is her preferred way to interact with mortals, as it helps her remain inconspicuous while still maintaining an air of otherworldly grace.  

***Fragmented Divinity.*** As a shard of Vaeil, Vaelithra wields divine power but cannot fully control it. Whenever she rolls a natural 1 on an attack roll, saving throw, or ability check, time shatters momentarily. Each creature within 30 feet must succeed on a DC 20 Wisdom saving throw or become stunned until the end of its next turn.  

***Innate Spellcasting.*** Vaelithra's spellcasting ability is Charisma spell save DC 21. She can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:  
At will: *hold person*, *slow*, *counterspell*  
3/day each: *banishment*, *greater restoration*, *wall of force*  
1/day each: *time stop*, *foresight*  

***Legendary Resistance (3/Day).*** If Vaelithra fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead.  

### Actions

 \columnbreak

***Multiattack.*** Vaelithra makes two attacks with her Chronal Strike.  

***Chronal Strike.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +11 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 18 (2d10 + 7) force damage plus 13 (3d8) radiant damage. On a hit, the target must succeed on a DC 21 Wisdom saving throw or lose its reaction until the start of its next turn.  

***Temporal Distortion.*** Vaelithra bends the flow of time in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a point she can see within 120 feet. Each creature in that area must succeed on a DC 21 Wisdom saving throw or be either aged (disadvantage on Strength and Dexterity checks and saving throws) or slowed (as the *slow* spell) for 1 minute. The effect ends early if Vaelithra loses concentration (as if concentrating on a spell).  

### Legendary Actions
*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, The vaelithra can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The vaelithra regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*

- **Detect.** Vaelithra makes a Wisdom (Perception) check.
- **Warp Step.** Vaelithra teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space she can see.
- **Chronal Glimpse (Costs 2 Actions).** Vaelithra forces a creature she can see within 30 feet to reroll an attack roll, saving throw, or ability check it just made. The creature must use the new roll.

## Xy'loth

**Alignment:** Chaotic Evil

**Domains:** Corruption, Chaos, Ruin

**Pantheon:** Demon Lords

**Symbol:** A stylized serpent intertwined with a shattered clock, its head poised to strike the center.

**Xyloth** is the primordial chaos that exists outside of time. It is not a being of flesh and blood but a malevolent force that seeks to unravel the orderly fabric of the multiverse, which was woven by **Vaeil**.

Unlike other demons who seek power or conquest, **Xyloth**'s sole purpose is the ultimate unmaking of all things, returning them to the chaotic void from which it came. Its influence is insidious, working through whispers and subversion rather than brute force.

**Xyloth** is the master of lies, twisting the faith of mortals, and turning order against itself. The **Acolyte of Silver Light** in your adventure secretly worships **Xyloth**, seeing its chaotic nature as true freedom from the gods' strictures. They believe that by bringing about the "*Ruin of Silver Light*," they are paving the way for **Xyloth**'s ultimate victory and the final liberation of all creation into pure chaos.
