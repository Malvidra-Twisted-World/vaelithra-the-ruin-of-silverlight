# Chapter 2: The Silver Forest

## Running This Chapter

This chapter shifts the adventure's tone entirely, moving from the safety of the tavern to the hostile tension of the woods. The Silver Forest is the players' first real taste of the corruption they are investigating, a place of twisted beauty that conceals a very real menace. This serves as a vital combat trial, meant to test your party's cohesion and combat strategy.

Encourage your players to explore the unsettling signs of corruption in the forest before the ambush. These clues are designed to build dread and foreshadow that something ancient and unnatural—the **Xy'loth**'s Pet—lingers deeper within.

## Entering the Forest

As the adventurers step beneath the forest canopy, they are met by an eerie, sudden stillness. The air grows heavy, muffling their footsteps and the patter of the rain, as if they've walked into a vacuum. The ancient trees and stones here are scarred with deep, jagged gashes, as though claws or blades have carved at the land itself. Strange silver motes drift through the branches, shimmering faintly before dissolving into nothingness when touched. Every now and then, a faint whisper—like a distorted prayer—echoes through the silence, carried on a non-existent breeze.

(Optional: A successful DC 15 Survival or Arcana check reveals that these marks are not random, but part of a sinister ritual, a deliberate defiling of the forest's natural life.)

## The Ambush at the Halfway Point

Just as the party nears the halfway point to the temple ruins, five figures melt from the shadows of the forest. They block the path ahead, their silver robes and gaunt faces a chilling mirror of the order they profess to serve.

- 4 **Acolyte of Silver Light**.
- 1 **Priest of Silver Light**.

Their goal is not to kill but to capture—they believe the adventurers are fated sacrifices or useful tools for their cause. The priest's eyes glow with a fanatic's devotion as he gestures, commanding his acolytes. "Chosen offerings," he declares, his voice a low hiss, "you walk willingly into our master's grace!"

- The acolytes and priest fight fiercely but with a reckless abandon. They prefer non-lethal strikes (such as the flats of their blades or blows to the head) to subdue the party, though their fanaticism may lead to lethal force if they are cornered. This combat will serve as a hard test of the party's skills.
- After a few rounds, the priest may use a spell to try and disorient the party, attempting to give the acolytes an advantage.

## Resolution

If the party is defeated, they will awaken bound and unconscious at the entrance to the ruins, starting Chapter 3 at a disadvantage. If the party wins, the priest is left critically wounded and can be interrogated.

- With a successful DC 15 Persuasion or Intimidation check, the priest, in his dying breaths, reveals:
  - A clear path to the Ruin of Silver Light, a twisted stairway covered in thorny vines.
  - A dark hint that the cult seeks to 'awaken their chained master'— **Xy'loth**'s Pet.
  - A final, chilling warning: "You think you walk toward salvation... but you walk willingly into the maw of eternity."

With the ambush resolved, the adventurers can now find the overgrown stairway that marks the temple entrance, where this chapter ends. The darkness of the forest gives way to the true corruption of the ruins.
