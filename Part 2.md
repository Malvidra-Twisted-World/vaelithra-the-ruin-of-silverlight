# Chapter 1: The Boar Smile Tavern

The adventure begins as the adventurers arrive in Dewanginokori under a torrential storm. Seeking warmth and shelter, they enter the Boar Smile Tavern, a bustling establishment packed full of travelers, locals, and laborers all waiting out the weather. With no empty tables left, the characters are forced to sit side by side at the bar, strangers drawn together by chance—or perhaps fate.

> [!quote] **Read Aloud Text**
>>It's Year 1 After Sundering, on the 1st of Aethelos, the village of Dewanginokori was silent as expected
>>
>>The rain pours heavily outside as thunder rattles the windows of the Boar Smile Tavern. Every table is full, leaving you no choice but to share one with strangers. The warmth of the hearth can't quite chase away the chill in the air, nor the uneasy whispers of the locals.
>>
>>A group of Silver Light acolytes sit near the bar, their silver-embroidered robes darkened with damp. Once revered as the city's blessing-givers, now they mutter among themselves, avoiding the gaze of common folk. A farmer leans in to whisper:
>>
>>"They don't bless the crops anymore. Those who beg their help vanish into the forest... and never come back."
>>

## Roleplaying the Scene

Encourage the players to introduce their characters: their weariness from travel, their reasons for being in Dewanginokori, and their reactions to being squeezed together. While they do, snippets of hushed conversation drift from the other customers. These whispers are the first hints that something is deeply wrong in the city.

## The Boar Smile Tavern NPCs

***The Bartender.*** The bartender on duty was an Elf named **Demolias Nogard**. With slightly disheveled blond hair and a pair of friendly green eyes, he greeted every newcomer with a warm smile. His build was unassuming, but his attire suggested a readiness for action: a white shirt and black vest paired with brown pants rolled up at the ankles. Your eye might be drawn to the sheathed shortsword on his back, a clear sign that he was more than just a man pouring drinks.

***The Server.*** The only server on duty was a woman with a commanding presence. Her name was **Diane Serpent**. She was notably tall with a strong, athletic build, and her brown hair was braided into two thick pigtails that swung slightly as she shifted her weight. Her uniform consisted of a tough, sleeveless gambeson over a loose-fitting tunic, and practical boots. The centerpiece of her gear, however, was a large, two-handed hammer that leaned against the wall next to her. She didn't seem to be in a rush, a silent guardian of the peace, ready to spring into action if needed.

### Rumors of the Silver Light

As your party settles into town, you can hear whispers about the local temple, the Silver Light. If you listen closely, or ask around, you might uncover some unsettling truths.

***A Missing Boy.*** You can try to catch the eye of a nervous-looking villager or simply listen in on a hushed conversation at the tavern.

- **Skill Check**: Make a DC 12 Perception or Insight check.
- **Success**: You overhear a man talking to a friend, his voice filled with worry. 'My son, Thomas, went into the Silver Forest days ago. They told me the Silver Light would watch over him, but he hasn't come back. I fear... I fear he's gone.'
- **Failure**: You only catch bits and pieces of the conversation. You hear the words 'missing' and 'Silver Forest', but you can't piece together the full story. The man's distress is clear, but the details are a blur.

***A Broken Worker.*** A local woodcutter, his clothes tattered and face pale, stumbles into the tavern, shaking and unable to speak. His eyes dart around the room in a panicked daze.

- **Skill Check**: Make a DC 14 Insight or Investigation check to try and figure out what happened to him.
- **Success**: You manage to get his attention and, with some gentle prompting, you learn he witnessed something terrible in the woods. He doesn't say what, but a single, terrifying word escapes his lips: 'They... changed.' He then collapses, exhausted.
- **Failure**: The man is too traumatized to respond. All you can discern is a deep, paralyzing fear. He stares blankly at the wall, unable to communicate his experience.

***A False Blessing.*** You hear whispers about a woman who rarely leaves her home anymore. It's said she had dealings with the Silver Light, but her story is a sad one. Finding her and convincing her to talk won't be easy.

- **Skill Check**: Make a DC 15 Persuasion or Intimidation check to get her to open up.
- **Success**: The woman, her eyes red from crying, tells you a sorrowful tale. Her brother was sick, and the Silver Light promised a cure if he came into their care. He vanished soon after, and she hasn't seen him since. She whispers, 'It wasn't a blessing. It was a lie.'
- **Failure**: She becomes frightened and pulls away, refusing to speak of her brother. Her distress is obvious, but you can't get her to reveal why she's so afraid.

These rumors paint a picture of unease and lost faith, setting suspicion on the Silver Light.

### A Call for Help

The rain continues to fall in a heavy curtain outside, a steady drumming on the tavern roof. The world inside is warm and filled with the low murmur of conversation and the clinking of mugs.

Suddenly, the heavy oak door bursts open, and a gust of cold, wet air rushes in, along with a man drenched to the bone. His eyes are wide with panic and his face is streaked with rain and tears. The sound of his desperate scramble into the room, followed by his raw scream, is what shatters the quiet, forcing every conversation to an abrupt halt.
> [!quote] **Read Aloud Text**
>>'Please!' he cries, his voice thick with terror. 'My sister—Elara! They took her!'
>>
>>He clutches his chest before forcing himself to stand tall. 'They wore long gray robes, and they dragged her into the Silver Forest toward the old temple! My name is Gareth Blackwood, and I'll give you everything I have—my family's entire inheritance! Just please, bring my sister Elara Blackwood home!'
>>
>>His desperate plea echoes through the sudden silence of the tavern. Every eye in the room—from the gruff dwarven merchant to the wary-looking barmaid—turns toward the man, and then to you. The weight of his hope and fear rests squarely on your shoulders.
>>

### Into the Storm

> [!quote] **Read Aloud Text**
>>The tension from Gareth's plea dissipates as he leads you out of the tavern and into the unforgiving storm. The town's meager lights disappear behind you as you approach the dark, foreboding edge of the Silver Forest.
>>
>>Here, at the treeline, Gareth stops. He turns to you, his face pale and eyes wide with terror, even more so than before.
>>
>>'I can't... I can't go in there,' he stammers, pointing a shaking hand into the shadowy trees. 'I... I can't lose her again. But please, adventurers, I beg of you. Don't let them take her light forever.'
>>
>>With that, he turns and flees back toward the safety of the town, leaving you alone at the mouth of the dark forest.
>>

As the party enters the woods, two robed figures rise from the shadows of the tavern and begin to follow from a distance. These are members of the corrupted Silver Light, sent to monitor or eliminate anyone who interferes with their plans. They should remain unseen for now.

A beggar woman, huddled outside the tavern, watches the adventurers leave. This is **Vaelithra**, a fragment of the god Vaeil. She is observing the party's first steps on their journey, but she will not interfere. Her presence is a subtle hint of a larger, divine conflict.
