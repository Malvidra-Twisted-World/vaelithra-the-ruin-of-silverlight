# Magic Items

#### Silverlight Amulet

Wondrous item, rare, 5 gp, ½ lb.

---

A pendant infused with the lingering blessing of Vaeil. While wearing this amulet, you gain **advantage** on saving throws against charm and frighten effects caused by fiends.

Once per long rest, you can channel the amulet’s power as an **Action** to cast *protection from evil and good* without expending a spell slot.

#### Fragment of the Archive

Wondrous item, rare, 5 gp, ⅕ lb.

---

A crystalline shard suffused with silvery light. This is a fragment of the **Vaeil**'s sundered soul — a **Vaelithra**.

While attuned, once per long rest, you can invoke its power to rewind a fleeting moment: when you or an ally within 30 feet fails a *Hit:*  saving throw, ability check, or attack roll, you can reroll it. The second result must be used.

If shattered intentionally, the fragment releases a surge of divine time-energy. Each ally within 20 feet may immediately take the Help action as a **Reaction**.

The fragment hums with faint whispers of archives unwritten.

#### Silver Charm

Wondrous item, common, 2 sp, 5 cp, 1.6 oz.

---

A small silver trinket carried by corrupted **Acolyte of Silver Light**.

When held, it grants **advantage** on a single *Hit:* saving throw against being frightened. The charm then loses its magic.

#### Blessed Candle

Wondrous item, common, 1 sp, ⅕ lb.

---

A white candle with faint silver runes.

When lit, it sheds bright light in a 10-foot radius and dim light for an additional 10 feet.

fiends within the bright light have **disadvantage** on Stealth checks.

#### Prayer Beads of Echo

Wondrous item, common, 1 sp, 5 cp, 4.8 oz.

---

A string of dull beads once used in prayer to Vaeil.

When used as a focus during a rest, it restores 1d4 temporary hit points to the bearer.

#### Silverleaf Ring

Wondrous item, common, 3 sp

---

A ring shaped like entwined silver leaves.

Once per day, you can reroll a 1d20 ability check related to Nature or Religion.

#### Whispering Feather

Wondrous item, common, 5 cp

---

A delicate silver-dyed feather.

When blown into the air, the bearer hears a whisper of guidance, adding 1d4 to their next ability check.

#### Dried Vial of Silver Fog

Wondrous item, common, 2 sp, 1.6 oz.

---

A tiny vial containing dried residue of the Silver Fog.

Smash it to create a 10-foot radius of lightly obscured mist for 1 minute.

#### Ashen Token

Wondrous item, common, 1 sp

---

A small disk of charred ash pressed into a token.

Once, you may automatically succeed on a single DC 10 Survival check.

#### Hollow Coin

Wondrous item, common, 1 cp

---

A strange coin with both sides hollowed out.

Before making a roll, you may flip the coin. Heads: +2 bonus. Tails: –2 penalty. You must accept the result.

#### Cracked Hourglass Pendant

Wondrous item, common, 3 sp, 5 cp, ⅕ lb.

---

A pendant shaped like a cracked hourglass.

Once per day, you can delay the effect of a condition (such as poisoned) for 1 minute before it takes hold.

#### Moonlit Thread

Wondrous item, common, 1 sp, 5 cp

---

A silvery thread that glimmers faintly in the moonlight.

When woven into clothing, it grants +1 AC for 1 hour after finishing a long rest. The thread is consumed after use.
